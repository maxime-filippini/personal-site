In teams where taking initiative is encouraged, the "techie" archetype tends to emerge. Someone who's interested in automating the boring stuff, and through that, provide value to the overall team. This tends to happen organically, because it is a fundamental element needed by every team.




## The techie's tool belt

Corporate environments are limited. Some enjoy more freedom than others, but **distribution** can become a key problem, since it is about providing access to these tools in a proper environment. Often, the techie's job will be limited to "maquettes". And once those are built, a dedicated IT team can take over. Most large asset management groups have those teams available for the manco in places like Poland or India, with links in the diverse jurisdictions etc.
