# Personal Publishing

This context describes the material published through Maxime's personal site and the states that material can occupy before and after publication.

## Language

**Post**:
An authored long-form work whose source may include prose and interactive elements.
_Avoid_: Article, document

**Draft**:
A post that is available for author review but is not linked or served as part of the public site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, private post

**Ready post**:
A validated post whose current revision is eligible for publication but has not necessarily been deployed and verified.
_Avoid_: Published post, finished draft

**Published post**:
A post included in the public site and reachable at its canonical URL.
_Avoid_: Live document

**Slug**:
The stable public identifier of a post. Once published, a slug changes only when the previous value is retained as a redirect.
_Avoid_: Filename, post ID

**Interactive element**:
A portion of a post that readers can manipulate, while the post retains a meaningful initial state without depending on live data.
_Avoid_: Widget, live content

**Post image**:
An image whose original source belongs to a post revision and whose delivered representations may vary by reader context.
_Avoid_: Uploaded image, image derivative

**Publication**:
The transition that makes a validated post part of the public site.
_Avoid_: Upload, refresh

**Runtime content**:
Mutable, non-executable information produced independently of site publication that the site may retrieve at request time.
_Avoid_: Dynamic post, live content
